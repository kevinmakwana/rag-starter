# Acme Cloud FAQ

Fictional product FAQ used as sample data for the RAG starter.

## Plans and pricing

Acme Cloud has three plans. Starter is free and includes one project and 1 GB of storage. Team costs $29 per user per month and includes unlimited projects and 100 GB of shared storage. Enterprise is priced per contract and adds SSO, audit logs and a 99.95% uptime SLA.

## Billing

Invoices are issued on the first day of each month and charged to the card on file. Annual billing gives two months free. Failed payments are retried three times over seven days before the workspace becomes read-only.

## Refund policy

You can cancel at any time. Monthly plans are not refunded for the current month, but you will not be charged again. Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within the first 30 days.

## Data and security

All data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Backups are taken every six hours and kept for 30 days. Customer data is stored in the EU region by default; the US region is available on Team and Enterprise plans.

## Support

Starter users get community support. Team users get email support with a one business day response time. Enterprise customers get a dedicated Slack channel and a four-hour response time for urgent issues.
